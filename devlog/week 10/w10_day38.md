Day 38:

Overview:
- Built the multi-metric evaluation harness: added F1 and AUC alongside accuracy
- Result: AUC ~0.50 at every degree = no discriminative power (the cleanest single-number "no signal" yet)

What I learned:
- F1 = harmonic mean of precision (when it says up, is it right?) and recall (does it catch the ups?). Only high when BOTH are decent, measures positive class quality, not just riding the majority
- AUC = area under the ROC curve = the probability the model ranks a random up-day above a random down-day. Threshold-independent, imbalance-proof. 0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect, <0.5 = inverted
- My results: AUC = [0.4999, 0.496, 0.490, 0.504] -> essentially 0.5 everywhere -> the model can't tell ups from downs at all
- F1 came out ~0.67, which LOOKS decent but is inflated by the ~54% up base rate (model predicts up a lot -> high recall, precision ~ base rate). A moderate F1 next to a coin-flip AUC is exactly why you report multiple metrics - F1 alone would mislead, AUC tells the truth
